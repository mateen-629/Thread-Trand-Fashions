Return Policy

30-Day Returns

We want you to love what you ordered. If something isn't right, you may return unworn, unwashed items with original tags attached within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to your original payment method.

How to Start a Return

  • Log into your account and select "Start a Return" on the relevant order
  • Choose whether you'd like a refund or an exchange for a different size or colour
  • Print the prepaid return label and drop your package at any carrier location

Items That Cannot Be Returned

  • Earrings and other pierced accessories, for hygiene reasons
  • Items marked "Final Sale" at time of purchase
  • Products showing signs of wear, washing, or altered tags

Refund Timeline

Once your return is received and inspected, refunds are processed within 5 business days. It may take an additional 3–5 business days for the refund to appear on your original payment method, depending on your bank.

Exchanges

Need a different size? Select the exchange option when starting your return and we'll ship the replacement as soon as your original item is scanned by the carrier — no need to wait for it to arrive back with us.

International Returns

International customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item arrived damaged, defective, or incorrect, in which case we'll cover the cost.

Questions?

Reach out any time via our Contact page or check our FAQ for quick answers.
