Our Story

Since 2019

Clothing built to be worn, not just bought.

Thread & Trend began in a single rented room above a fabric wholesaler in New York's Garment District, with one idea: make fewer, better pieces from materials that actually hold up. No seasonal gimmicks — just wool, cotton, linen and leather, cut well and finished properly.

Seven years on, we still work with many of the same small mills and family-run workshops we started with. Every piece is produced in limited runs, which means less waste, tighter quality control, and a collection that doesn't try to be everything to everyone.

Our journey

Milestones

2019

A single rack, one collection

Thread & Trend launches with an 18-piece outerwear and knitwear capsule, sold directly to 30 customers in New York.

2021

Kids & Accessories added

We expand beyond ready-to-wear into considered basics for children and a small leather goods line.

2023

Carbon-neutral shipping

Every order begins shipping carbon-neutral as standard, at no extra cost to the customer.

2026

40,000 customers, one philosophy

Still small-batch, still made from natural fibres, still designed to be worn for years — not one season.
