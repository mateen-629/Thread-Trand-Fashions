Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you browse, register, or place an order with Thread & Trend, we collect information such as your name, email address, shipping address, phone number and payment details. We also collect basic usage data — pages visited, items viewed, and device type — to help us improve the site.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process orders, provide customer support, personalise your shopping experience, and — where you've opted in — send marketing communications about new arrivals and promotions. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Cookies

We use cookies to remember items in your bag and wishlist, keep you signed in, and understand how visitors use our site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function correctly as a result.

4. Data Sharing

We share information only with trusted service providers who help us operate the business — such as payment processors and shipping carriers — and only to the extent necessary for them to perform their services.

5. Your Rights

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time via the unsubscribe link

6. Data Security

All payment information is processed through encrypted, PCI-compliant channels. We maintain administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect your data from unauthorised access.

7. Contact Us

Questions about this policy can be directed to privacy@threadandtrend.com or via our Contact page.
